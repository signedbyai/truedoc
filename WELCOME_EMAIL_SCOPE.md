## What this replaces

SignedBy already sends an email at the exact moment Lemonade's fires — right
after a purchase completes. It's `sendPlanUpgradeEmail` in `src/lib/email.ts`,
triggered from the `checkout.session.completed` handler in
`src/app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts`. Today it's four lines of plain text:
a thank-you line and a "Go to your dashboard" button. This scope upgrades
that same send — same trigger, same call site, no new webhook or migration —
into the fuller welcome treatment, matching the beats of the Lemonade email
(congratulations framing, a visual moment, what's included, what to do if
something goes wrong, sign-off).

Two things already wired into that send stay untouched: the Trustpilot AFS
BCC (`trustpilotAfsBcc()` — copies Trustpilot's review-invite trigger) and
the fact that it only fires on a *new* subscription, not every renewal.

## Content plan (mapped from the Lemonade example)

| Lemonade beat | SignedBy equivalent |
|---|---|
| "Congrats! Here is your new policy" | "You're in — welcome to SignedBy [Plan]" |
| "Hey Michael, Great to have you with us!" | Same tone, personalized to the org/account name already passed into `sendPlanUpgradeEmail` |
| Team photo, animated | Animated SignedBy wordmark/mark (see below) |
| "I've attached your new policy along with the rest of your docs" | Not a literal attachment — link to the dashboard, since there's no document yet on day one. Instead: a short 2–3 step "what to do first" list (e.g. send your first document, invite a teammate, turn on the Verified Badge) |
| "If anything bad should happen, just open the Lemonade app and get our help" | Point at the existing in-app feedback widget (nav message-bubble icon, already built — `sendFeedbackEmail`) rather than promising a new support channel |
| "Stay safe, Maya — Team Lemonade" | Sign-off from SignedBy, not a fabricated "team" signature — see brand note below |

One deliberate departure from Lemonade: their email is signed by "Maya" with
a named human face on the photo. SignedBy doesn't have a support team to
personify that way yet, so the sign-off should stay as the SignedBy brand,
not an invented person — inventing a "Maya" would be a trust problem the
moment a reply or a support ticket doesn't match.

## The animated visual

No team photo exists to animate (checked `public/brand/` — logo lockups
only, no photography). Per your steer, this becomes an animated SignedBy
mark instead: a short GIF of the wordmark/slash-icon drawing itself in, or a
signature stroke animating across the page — same spirit as Lemonade's
"clapping team" (a moment of warmth/motion at the top of the email) without
needing a photo shoot.

Production path: reuse the pipeline already proven on this project for
motion-adjacent brand assets — HTML/CSS or SVG built to the exact brand hex
and `Inter` font-family (see `linkedin-company-page-banner.md`'s documented
gotchas), rendered frame-by-frame via Playwright, assembled into a GIF
(`gifski` or `ffmpeg`). No new tooling needed; the sandbox already lacks a
root/Chromium requirement workaround from that build.

**Deliverability catch:** Outlook desktop (the Word rendering engine) shows
only the *first frame* of an animated GIF, not the animation. So the first
frame has to be a complete, intentional image on its own — e.g. the
wordmark fully drawn — not a blank canvas the animation would otherwise
build up from. This needs to be true by construction, not fixed after the
fact.

**Hosting:** the GIF needs a public URL for the `<img>` tag — every other
email in `email.ts` is plain HTML with no images today, so this is the
first email to need one. Same pattern as the Reddit/LinkedIn ad creatives:
host on R2 or `public/` served from `signedby.ai`, not inlined.

## What this does NOT touch

- No new database migration — this is a template/content change to an
  existing send.
- No new vendor or cost — Resend is already paid for; the GIF is a one-time
  design asset, not a recurring expense.
- Doesn't change the free-signup flow at all (per your steer, this fires on
  paid subscription only — Supabase Auth's magic-link email for plain
  signup is untouched).
