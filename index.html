What a pass actually means. The file is byte-for-byte identical to the one that was signed or timestamped at the moment shown, and that signature checks out. Change a single byte and it fails.

What it does not mean. A timestamp records when, never who. A signature proves which certificate was used, which is a fact about a key rather than a verified fact about a person. Neither can tell you the document is honest, that the sender is who they say, or that the payment details on it are right. Confirm those the way you always would.

Unrecognised authority is not a failure. This page pins a deliberately short list of roots so the list stays checkable by a human. Most documents from elsewhere will say the authority is unrecognised; that means confirm it yourself, not that anything is wrong.
